## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

**Not applicable: PanelMaster draws no title-bar controls of its own.** Every surface of the
addon's own was checked:

- the panels (`modules/Canvas.lua`): the player's content, with no chrome at all;
- the unlock overlay (`modules/Unlock.lua`): an outline and a centered name label on a
  mouse-transparent child frame, with no buttons;
- the settings pages (`settings/`): the library's Options shell, whose title-bar controls are the
  library's (`SR-L2`, `SR-L3`); the one `AceGUI` Button in `settings/Panel.lua` is a page control,
  not a title-bar control;
- `NS.MakeCloseButton` (`core/CoreSetup.lua`) has no caller in the addon; it only passes the owner
  through to `lib.MakeCloseButton`, so any future close inherits the library's tooltip and colors.

The windows with title bars a player sees (the debug console, copy window, sim and perf panels,
skin picker) are the library's and arrive with the re-copy. No `W.HeaderButtons` adoption and no
new test, since there is no addon-owned control to test.
